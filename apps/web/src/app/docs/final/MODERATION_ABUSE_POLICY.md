## 🚨 ABUSE SCENARIO CATEGORIES

### **1. FAKE SELLER / FRAUDULENT PRODUCTS**

#### **Scenario: Seller lists counterfeit or non-existent products**

**Detection Methods:**
- 🤖 **AI Analysis:** Image reverse search for stock photos, price anomaly detection
- 👥 **User Reports:** "Counterfeit product" or "Item not received" flags
- 📊 **Pattern Recognition:** Multiple refund requests, low ratings, rapid listing volume
- 🔍 **Manual Review:** Random seller verification checks

**Investigation Protocol:**

| **Step** | **Action** | **Timeline** | **Responsible Team** |
|----------|-----------|--------------|---------------------|
| **1. Alert Triggered** | Flag seller account, freeze payouts | Immediate | Automated System |
| **2. Evidence Collection** | Gather product listings, customer complaints, order history | 24 hours | Fraud Team |
| **3. Seller Contact** | Request documentation (invoices, authenticity certificates) | 48 hours | Seller Support |
| **4. Investigation** | Verify documents, check supplier legitimacy | 3-5 days | Compliance Team |
| **5. Decision** | Suspend, restrict, or clear seller | Within 7 days | Senior Moderator |

**Possible Outcomes:**

| **Evidence Level** | **Action** | **Customer Protection** |
|-------------------|-----------|------------------------|
| **Confirmed Counterfeit** | Permanent ban, refund all orders, report to authorities | Full refund + compensation |
| **Misleading Listings** | 30-day suspension, mandatory training, listing review | Full refund |
| **Unresponsive Seller** | Account freeze, auto-refunds initiated | Full refund after 7 days |
| **False Accusation** | Seller cleared, reporter educated | No action |

**Customer Communication:**
```
Subject: Update on Your Order #EZY1234567

Dear [Customer Name],

We've completed our investigation regarding your report about [Product Name].

Our findings: [Confirmed counterfeit / Seller unable to verify authenticity]

Actions taken:
✅ Seller account permanently banned
✅ Full refund processed ($XXX.XX)
✅ Compensation credit added ($XX.XX)
✅ Product listings removed

Your refund will appear in 3-5 business days.

We're sorry for this experience and have strengthened our seller verification.

- EZYIFY Trust & Safety Team
```

### **2. CREATOR POLICY VIOLATIONS**

#### **Scenario A: Creator promotes unsafe/illegal products in content**

**Examples:**
- Promoting counterfeit designer items
- Advertising prescription drugs without license
- Encouraging dangerous "challenges"
- Undisclosed sponsored content

**Detection:**
- 🤖 AI keyword detection in captions
- 👥 User reports ("Dangerous content", "Scam promotion")
- 🔍 Manual creator audits (top creators reviewed quarterly)

**Enforcement Ladder:**

| **Violation Type** | **First Offense** | **Second Offense** | **Final Offense** |
|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Undisclosed Sponsorship** | Warning + content flagged | 7-day monetization freeze | Demonetization |
| **Promoting Counterfeits** | Content removed + warning | 14-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| **Dangerous Content** | Content removed + 7-day ban | 30-day ban | Permanent ban |
| **Illegal Products** | **Immediate permanent ban + law enforcement report** |

**Appeal Process:**
1. Creator receives detailed violation email
2. 14-day window to submit appeal with evidence
3. Independent reviewer (different from original moderator) reviews
4. Decision final within 7 days
5. If overturned, record expunged and content restored

---

#### **Scenario B: Creator harassment or hate speech**

**Definition:**
- Targeted attacks based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation
- Coordinated harassment campaigns
- Doxxing (sharing private information)

**Zero-Tolerance Protocol:**
1. **Immediate Action:** Content removed, creator account suspended
2. **Notification:** Victims notified, offered resources (counseling, legal support)
3. **Investigation:** Review all creator content for pattern of behavior
4. **Outcome:** Permanent ban (no appeal for severe cases)
5. **Law Enforcement:** Reported if threats of violence detected

### **3. LIVE STREAM ABUSE HANDLING**

#### **Scenario: Inappropriate content during live stream**

**Types of Abuse:**
- Nudity or sexual content
- Extreme violence or gore
- Hate speech or harassment
- Copyright violations (playing music, showing movies)

**Real-Time Moderation System:**

| **Detection Method** | **Response Time** | **Action** |
|---------------------|------------------|-----------|
| **AI Video Analysis** | < 5 seconds | Auto-pause stream, notify moderator |
| **User Reports** (5+ reports) | < 30 seconds | Human moderator joins stream |
| **Keyword Triggers** | Instant | Warning overlay to streamer |

**Live Stream Suspension Flow:**
```
1. Warning (yellow banner): "Content violates guidelines. Correct or stream will end."
2. Grace Period: 30 seconds to comply
3. Auto-End: Stream terminated, 10-minute cooldown
4. Review: Moderator reviews recording
5. Decision: No penalty / Warning / Suspension / Ban
```

**Streamer Notification:**
```
Your live stream was ended due to [violation type].

What happened: [Specific issue, timestamp]
Guidelines violated: [Link to relevant policy]

Next steps:
- Review our Community Guidelines
- Complete mandatory training module
- Cooldown period: 24 hours

Appeals: If you believe this was an error, submit an appeal within 14 days.
```

**Viewer Refund Policy:**
- Gifts sent in last 5 minutes before termination: Auto-refunded
- Viewers notified of violation and refund

### **4. REFUND & DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

#### **Dispute Type 1: Item Not Received**

**Customer Claims Timeline:**

| **Day** | **Customer Action** | **System Action** |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Order Placed** | Waits for delivery | Tracking updates sent |
| **Expected Delivery +3** | Still not received | Auto-reminder to seller |
| **+7 days** | Opens "Item Not Received" case | Seller has 48 hours to respond |
| **+10 days** | Escalates to dispute | EZYIFY investigates |

**Evidence Required:**
- **Customer:** Order confirmation, delivery address verification
- **Seller:** Tracking number with delivery confirmation, shipping receipt

**Resolution Matrix:**

| **Scenario** | **Outcome** | **Refund** | **Seller Impact** |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|------------------|
| **No tracking provided** | Customer wins | Full refund | Seller warned, funds held |
| **Tracking shows delivered** | Seller wins | No refund | Customer advised to check neighbors |
| **Tracking shows in transit** | Extended time | Partial refund (50%) | Seller must reship or full refund |
| **Seller unresponsive** | Customer wins (auto) | Full refund + $10 credit | Account suspended |

---

#### **Dispute Type 2: Item Not As Described**

**Examples:**
- Wrong color/size received
- Product damaged or defective
- Significant quality difference from photos

**Resolution Process:**

**Step 1: Customer Opens Dispute (within 7 days of delivery)**
- Provides photos/video of received item
- Describes discrepancy

**Step 2: Seller Response (48 hours)**
- Option A: Accept return, issue refund
- Option B: Dispute claim with counter-evidence
- Option C: Offer partial refund

**Step 3: EZYIFY Mediation (if no agreement)**
```
Evidence Review:
✅ Product listing description & photos
✅ Customer photos of received item
✅ Seller's explanation
✅ Product review history (other customers)

Decision Criteria:
- Material difference = Full refund + return shipping
- Minor difference = Partial refund (20-50%)
- False claim = Dispute denied, customer warned
```

**Return Shipping:**
- Seller's fault: Seller pays return shipping
- Customer's mistake: Customer pays return shipping
- Defective product: EZYIFY covers return shipping

---

#### **Dispute Type 3: Counterfeit Product Claims**

**High-Risk Process (Brand Protection):**

1. **Escalation:** Automatically escalated to specialist team
2. **Seller Investigation:** Request supplier invoices, purchase receipts
3. **Brand Verification:** Contact brand owner if possible (for major brands)
4. **Expert Review:** Third-party authentication service (if high-value)

**If Confirmed Counterfeit:**
- ✅ Customer: Full refund + $50 compensation
- ❌ Seller: Permanent ban, all earnings forfeited
- 🚔 Report to brand owner and law enforcement
- 📧 Notify all customers who purchased from seller (with refund offers)

### **6. PAYMENT DISPUTES & CHARGEBACKS**

#### **Chargeback Handling**

**When Customer Files Chargeback:**

**Step 1: Notification (within 24 hours)**
- Email to seller with chargeback details
- Funds immediately held from seller account

**Step 2: Evidence Submission (7 days)**
Seller must provide:
- Proof of delivery (tracking + signature)
- Product description accuracy proof
- Customer communication logs
- Photos of shipped item (if available)

**Step 3: Representment to Bank**
- EZYIFY compiles evidence package
- Submits to payment processor / bank
- Timeline: 30-90 days for decision

**Outcomes:**

| **Bank Decision** | **Seller Impact** | **Customer Impact** |
|-------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Seller Wins** | Funds released | Customer charged again |
| **Customer Wins** | Funds deducted + $15 chargeback fee | Keeps product + refund |
| **Partial Win** | Partial refund | Partial refund kept |

**Chargeback Fraud Detection:**
- Customer with 3+ chargebacks in 6 months: Payment methods disabled
- Must use "Buy Now, Pay Later" or cash on delivery only

### **7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (COPYRIGHT / TRADEMARK) VIOLATIONS**

#### **DMCA Takedown Process**

**Copyright Claim Flow:**

1. **Rights Holder Files DMCA Notice:**
   - Identify copyrighted work
   - URL of infringing content
   - Contact information
   - Good faith statement

2. **EZYIFY Response (within 24 hours):**
   - Content removed or access disabled
   - User notified of takedown

3. **Counter-Notification (User has 10 days):**
   - User can dispute if they believe claim is invalid
   - Provides counter-evidence

4. **Restore or Permanent Removal:**
   - If no counter-notice: Content stays down
   - If counter-notice filed: Rights holder must sue within 14 days or content restored

**Repeat Infringer Policy:**
- **Strike 1:** Content removed, warning issued
- **Strike 2:** 7-day suspension, copyright training required
- **Strike 3:** Permanent account termination

---

#### **Trademark Violations (Counterfeit Goods)**

**Brand Protection Program:**
- Verified brands can enroll in Brand Registry
- Automated detection of unauthorized use of trademarks
- Expedited takedown process (within 4 hours)

**Seller Consequences:**
- First offense: All infringing listings removed
- Second offense: Account suspended pending investigation
- Confirmed counterfeit: Permanent ban

### **8. ACCOUNT RECOVERY & APPEALS**

#### **Wrongful Suspension Appeal**

**Appeal Submission Requirements:**
```
1. Detailed explanation of why suspension is incorrect
2. Supporting evidence (screenshots, receipts, etc.)
3. Acknowledgment of guidelines (to prevent future issues)
4. Promise to comply with policies going forward
```

**Review Process:**
- Assigned to different moderator than original decision
- Independent review within 5 business days
- Decision is final (no further appeals)

**Outcome Statistics (for transparency):**
- ~15% of appeals overturned
- ~10% result in reduced penalties
- ~75% upheld as originally decided

---

#### **Account Hacking / Unauthorized Access**

**Recovery Protocol:**

**User Reports:** "My account was hacked"

**Step 1: Immediate Security:**
- Force logout on all devices
- Disable password reset via email
- Flag account for review

**Step 2: Identity Verification:**
- Photo ID upload
- Answer security questions
- Email verification from original registration email
- SMS code to original phone number

**Step 3: Account Review:**
- Check for unauthorized purchases
- Review content posted during suspected hack
- Refund fraudulent transactions
- Remove violating content

**Step 4: Restoration:**
- Password reset via verified email
- Enable 2FA (mandatory going forward)
- Activity log provided to user
- Security recommendations

### **9. CRISIS RESPONSE PROTOCOLS**

#### **Scenario: Viral Misinformation or Dangerous Content**

**Example:** Viral post promoting harmful "challenge" or false health claims

**Rapid Response Team Activation:**

| **Timeline** | **Action** | **Team** |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| **0-15 min** | Identify trending harmful content | Community Managers |
| **15-30 min** | Remove original post, flag related content | Moderation Team |
| **30-60 min** | Deploy AI filter to prevent reposts | Engineering |
| **1-2 hours** | Public statement on safety channels | Communications |
| **24 hours** | Partner with fact-checkers / health authorities | Leadership |

**User Communication:**
```
🚨 Important Safety Alert

We've identified and removed content promoting [dangerous activity/false information].

What we're doing:
✅ All related posts removed
✅ Users who engaged receive safety information
✅ Enhanced detection deployed

Your safety is our priority. If you see similar content, report it immediately.

Resources: [Link to safety info / fact-check]
```

#### **Scenario: Platform Outage During High-Traffic Event (e.g., Big Sale)**

**Incident Management:**

1. **Detection:** Monitoring alerts on performance degradation
2. **Assessment:** Determine cause (server, DDoS, traffic spike)
3. **Communication:** Status page updated + in-app banner
4. **Mitigation:** Scale servers, enable queue system
5. **Resolution:** Fix implemented, services restored
6. **Post-Mortem:** Incident report, compensation for affected users

**User Compensation (if outage > 1 hour during sale):**
- ✅ Extend sale period by outage duration
- ✅ $10 credit to active shoppers during outage
- ✅ Priority access when services resume

### **10. DATA BREACH RESPONSE**

#### **Protocol for Security Incident**

**Discovery → Containment → Assessment → Notification → Resolution**

| **Phase** | **Timeline** | **Actions** |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Containment** | 0-1 hour | Isolate affected systems, halt data flow |
| **Assessment** | 1-4 hours | Determine scope: what data, how many users |
| **Notification (Internal)** | 4-8 hours | Inform executive team, legal, PR |
| **Notification (Regulatory)** | Within 72 hours | GDPR/CCPA required notifications |
| **Notification (Users)** | Within 72 hours | Email to affected users |
| **Resolution** | Ongoing | Patch vulnerability, enhance security |
| **Post-Mortem** | 30 days | Public transparency report |

**User Notification Template:**
```
Subject: Important Security Update for Your EZYIFY Account

Dear [Name],

We're writing to inform you of a security incident that may have affected your account.

What happened: [Brief description]
Data potentially affected: [Email / Name / etc. — NOT passwords or payment info]
What we're doing: [Security measures implemented]

What you should do:
1. Reset your password immediately [Link]
2. Enable 2-Factor Authentication [Link]
3. Monitor your account for suspicious activity

We sincerely apologize and are committed to preventing this in the future.

Compensation: We've added $25 credit to your account as an apology.

Questions? Contact: security@ezyify.app

- EZYIFY Security Team
```
